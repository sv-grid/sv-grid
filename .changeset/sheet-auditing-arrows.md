Formula auditing: Trace Precedents, Trace Dependents and Remove Arrows on
Formulas > Formula Auditing. Excel's blue arrows, a dot on the cell read
and an arrowhead on the formula, drawn over the rendered cells and
following every scroll; each press traces one more level out. The
workbook gains `precedents(sheet, row, col)` and `dependents(sheet, row,
col)`, read from its dependency graph.
