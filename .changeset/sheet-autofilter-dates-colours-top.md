AutoFilter: Date Filters, Filter by Color and Top 10.

A column of dates offers Excel's periods (Today, This Week, Last Month,
This Quarter, Year to Date and the rest, weeks Sunday to Saturday) and
Equals, Before, After and Between with a typed date. A column whose cells
carry more than one fill, its own or a conditional format's, offers Filter
by Color with a swatch per fill and No Fill. A column of numbers offers
Top 10: top or bottom, so many items or so many percent, counted over the
whole column. The filters ride in the document as `date`, `color` and
`top`; `hiddenRowsFor` takes a `fillAt` reader and a `today`, and
`datePeriodBounds` is the calendar on its own.
