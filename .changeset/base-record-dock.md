Base: a record opens in the middle, beside the table or full page, picked with three buttons in its header and kept on the device. Beside the table it is a panel that leaves the grid usable: the base gives up its width, another row picked in the grid shows in it, and its edge sets the width. `recordDock`, `setRecordDock`, `recordDockWidth` and `setRecordDockWidth` are exported. SvModal takes `fill` (the whole window) and a `headerEnd` snippet.
