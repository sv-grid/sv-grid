Base: a table's `color` tints its tab and colours its name. `tablesAt="left"` lists the tables on the left of the base, under folding headings from each table's `section` (remembered folding, Up and Down through the list, "Put under a heading" in the table menu, a switch in the list of tables); `onTablesAt` and `onTableSection` tell the app what to keep. A base narrower than 760 px keeps tabs.
