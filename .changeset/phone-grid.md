Grid: `maxPinnedColumnWidth` draws a pinned column no wider than that (its
own width is kept and comes back when the limit goes). SvBase:
`phoneGrid="grid"` keeps a grid view a grid on a phone, its pinned first
field at most about two fifths of the width, with "+" to add a record; the
other views keep their phone layouts.
