SvGrid Base colors cells by rule: a view's `cellColors` tint a field's cell
while it matches a condition on that field (the view bar's Color, Cells).
"Mark records by hand" adds a Highlight single select and colors rows by it.
Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U and Ctrl+1 in a base say where colors come from.
