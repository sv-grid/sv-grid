Base: "Customize board" on a Kanban view: rows (swimlanes) by a second single select or person field, up to four fields on the cards, a due date badge that turns red once the day has passed, cards that fade as a date (a Last modified time) gets older, a blocked mark from a checkbox, select fields as quick filter chips above the board, and a card limit per column.
